PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

End-to-End Forensic Integrity & Controlled Erasure

Designed strictly to solve SIH26149 by bridging the technical gap between evidentiary recovery, chain-of-custody tracking, and verifiable device sanitization.

Cryptographic Custody

Instant SHA-256 calculation upon ingest into controlled storage. Continuous verification guarantees no file is modified during analysis.

Multi-Format Signature Carving

Deterministic byte-level boundary scanning for documents, archives, and images from unallocated clusters and memory dumps.

Two-Person Sanitization Gate

Destructive zero overwrite execution is procedurally and cryptographically locked until legal hold is released and two distinct officers authorize.

Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger

Every action is recorded in a cryptographically chained block structure with previous/current hashes, preventing backdated modifications.

Activity Timeline Reconstruction

Automated chronological event normalization mapping case creation, device ingestion, carving jobs, approvals, and sanitizations.

Verifiable PDF Certificates

Generates official sanitization certificates embedded with cryptographic digests, approval records, and scannable QR verification tokens.
