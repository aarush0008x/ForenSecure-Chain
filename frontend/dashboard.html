How ForenSecure Chain Works & Operational Guide

The 8-Step Forensic Examination Lifecycle (Steps to Use)

Follow this standardized operating procedure for all evidentiary acquisitions and data sanitization tasks.

STEP 01

Register Case Docket & Legal Hold

Initialize a new case docket with official case number, incident description, and assigned Lead Examiner. Ensure Legal Hold Policy is activated to prevent premature erasure during legal discovery.

STEP 02

Register Physical Storage Hardware

Catalog target hardware (HDDs, SSDs, flash memory, smartphones) with model identifier and physical serial numbers. Calculate and record the physical bitstream baseline SHA-256 hash.

STEP 03

Ingest Evidence Files & Lock Baseline Hash

Upload forensic raw disk images or file artifacts. The engine computes SHA-256 fingerprints in real time, places files in isolated write-protected storage, and commits an immutable acquisition block to the ledger.

STEP 04

Execute Deep File Carving & Recovery

Analyze raw bytes to extract deleted, damaged, or unallocated files using signature carving (JPEG, PNG, PDF, ZIP, GIF). Carved items are cataloged separately without modifying primary evidence.

STEP 05

Inspect Event Reconstruction Timeline

Review the chronological activity timeline to correlate suspect actions, file timestamps, evidence access logs, and legal hold state transitions with forensic precision.

STEP 06

Release Legal Hold & Request Sanitization

When investigation concludes or retention period expires, toggle Legal Hold to Released and submit a formal media sanitization request citing regulatory or decommissioning grounds.

STEP 07

Dual-Officer Independent Authorization

Two distinct Authorized Officers review the justification in the Approvals Queue. The requester is barred from self-approval. Both signatures are recorded permanently on the blockchain ledger.

STEP 08

Sanitize, Verify & Export Court Dossier

Execute NIST SP 800-88 cryptographic wipe. The engine re-reads storage to verify zero entropy, mints a verifiable QR certificate, and generates official 3-page Case Evidence Dossiers.

ForenSecure Chain — Target Architecture (SIH26149)

ForenSecure Chain provides an end-to-end digital forensics and data sanitization lifecycle:

Case Registration → Evidence Registration → Forensic Acquisition → SHA-256 Integrity → Analysis & Recovery → Activity Timeline → Evidence Report → Legal Closure → Two-Person Approval → Sanitization → Verification → Certificate → Immutable Audit Record
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict server-side separation for Investigator, Authorized Officer, and Admin roles.
  • Two-Person Approval Gate: Zero destructive overwriting permitted until two distinct officers sign off.
  • Tamper-Evident Ledger: SHA-256 hash-chained local ledger with cryptographic integrity recalculation.
  • Verifiable PDF Certificates: Embedded QR verification tokens linking to immutable ledger blocks.
